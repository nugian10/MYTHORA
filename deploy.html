Deploy the Mythora Contract

This page sends the Mythora contract to BNB Smart Chain. Only your wallet signs, inside your own wallet app.

Two different addresses. The wallet you connect becomes the contract owner. Mint proceeds go to a separate payout address that is fixed in the contract.

Important. This page NEVER asks for a seed phrase or private key. Never type a seed phrase or private key into any web page, and never send one to anyone, including Muse. If a page asks for one, it is a scam. Close that page.
